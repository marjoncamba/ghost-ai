- Tiny black gap at the bottom corners of the Clerk card, between the form section and the footer ("Don't have an account?"). Root cause (found by enumerating matched CSS rules): `.cl-cardBox` is a transparent `overflow: hidden` clipping mask rounded to `calc(var(--radius-2xl) * 2)` — the actual fill colors come from its children `.cl-card` (form section) and `.cl-footer`, and `.cl-footer` had `border-radius: 0`.
